Most small business websites lose customers in the same few places.

People decide in seconds. If they can’t see what you offer, or contacting you takes effort, they go to the next business on Google.

Check my website for free
  • Unclear offerVisitors can’t tell what you sell, where you are or why they should choose you.
  • Hard to contactCalling, WhatsApp or directions take more than one tap, so people give up.
  • Slow on phonesMost of your visitors are on a phone. A slow or broken mobile site loses them first.
  • Invisible on GoogleYou don’t show up when people nearby search for what you sell.
  • Stuck without a developerPrices, products and photos go out of date because updating them is a hassle.

Case study · Lbloom garden center, Tbilisi

From a new store with no online presence to a site, catalog and content that bring people in.

The challenge
A new garden center in Tbilisi with hundreds of plants and garden furniture, and no way to show them online or reach people beyond those passing by.
What we did
Custom website with plant and furniture catalogs, garden stories and an admin system the team updates on its own. Plus the ongoing marketing: short videos, social media and the content behind them.
The result
Lbloom’s Reels have passed 380K views on a page with 6.3K followers, and the site gives every viewer one place to see the range and get in touch.
